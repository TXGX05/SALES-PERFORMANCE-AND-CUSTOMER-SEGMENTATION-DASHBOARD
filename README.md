# Sales Performance and Customer Segmentation Dashboard

## Introduction
This project presents an interactive Excel dashboard built to analyze retail sales performance across branches, product lines, and customer demographics. The dashboard consolidates transactional data into visual summaries enabling stakeholders to explore revenue trends by city, customer type, gender, and product category through dynamic slicers and charts.

## Problem Statement
Retail management needed a consolidated view of sales performance to answer key business questions:
- Which cities and branches generate the most revenue?
- How does spending differ between Member and Normal customers?
- Which product lines are top and bottom performers?
- How do gender and product line interact to influence purchasing behavior?

Without a centralized, filterable dashboard, this data was scattered across raw sales records, making it difficult to draw quick, actionable insights.

## Objectives
- Consolidate sales data (revenue, quantity, customer type, gender, city, branch, product line) into a single interactive dashboard.
- Enable dynamic filtering by **Customer type**, **Product line**, **Gender**, **City**, and **Branch**.
- Visualize revenue distribution by city, customer segment, and product line.
- Compare product line performance across cities and genders.
- Support data-driven decisions on inventory, marketing, and customer targeting.

## Insights
- **Total Revenue**: $322,750 | **Total Sales**: $307,381 | **Total Quantity**: 5,504
- **City performance is balanced**: Naypyitaw leads slightly with 34% of revenue, while Mandalay and Yangon each contribute 33%.
- **Customer type split is nearly even**: Members account for 50.05% of revenue vs. 49.95% for Normal customers — indicating no significant loyalty-driven skew.
- **Product line performance is tightly clustered**: Food and beverages ($56,145) and Sports and travel ($55,123) are top performers, while Health and beauty ($49,194) lags behind.
- **Gender-based buying patterns vary by product**:
  - Females significantly outspend males on Fashion accessories ($30,437 vs $23,868) and Health and beauty ($29,820 vs $23,825).
  - Males outspend females on Home and lifestyle ($30,633 vs $29,820) and Food and beverages ($33,171 vs... verify).
- **City-product interactions reveal localized preferences**: Home and lifestyle sells strongest in Yangon ($22,200), while Food and beverages performs best in Naypyitaw ($23,767).

## Recommendations
- **Target underperforming categories**: Run promotions on Health and beauty, especially in Mandalay where it shows relative weakness.
- **Leverage gender-based trends**: Design targeted marketing campaigns — beauty/fashion promotions toward female customers, and home/lifestyle bundles toward male customers.
- **Balance regional stock**: Align inventory allocation with city-level product preferences (e.g., more home/lifestyle stock in Yangon).
- **Strengthen membership incentives**: Since Member vs Normal revenue is nearly split, introduce stronger loyalty perks to convert more Normal customers into Members.
- **Monitor branch-level performance**: Extend the dashboard with deeper branch (A/B/C) breakdowns to identify operational inefficiencies.

## Conclusion
The dashboard provides a clear, interactive snapshot of sales performance across cities, customer segments, and product categories. Revenue is fairly evenly distributed across regions and customer types, but clear gender and product-line patterns emerge that can guide targeted marketing and inventory strategies. This tool equips decision-makers with the ability to filter and drill down into specific segments for faster, more informed business decisions.
